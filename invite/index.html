Accept your invitation

Sign in with the email address the invitation was sent to. Archon matches your invitation when you sign in, so there is nothing to enter here.

Sign-in goes through Auth0, which lets you continue with Google or GitHub. Use the same address the owner invited — an invitation is matched against an address your provider has verified, so signing in with a different one will not find it.

Sign in to Archon

After signing in, open the document link the owner sent you. If you were invited some time ago, ask the owner to invite you again: an invitation expires.

An older invitation email may link here with a setup token in the address bar. That token no longer does anything and can be ignored — signing in is all that is needed.
