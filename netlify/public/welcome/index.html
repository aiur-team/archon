Welcome to Archon.

Archon turns an artifact into a real document with auth, editing and live collaboration. Here is the whole flow, start to finish.

  1. 1

    Tell your agent:

    > Turn my artifact into an Archon doc: https://archon.aiur.team
  2. 2

    Your agent redirects you to authorize and uploads your artifact to Archon.

  3. 3

    Share your doc with others and collaborate.

Waiting on an approval? Your agent's publications are on your pending publications, or you can type the pairing code it gave you.
